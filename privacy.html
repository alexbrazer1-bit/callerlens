CallerLens privacy policy

Last updated 1 November 2026.

CallerLens is a phone app. It shows who is calling, keeps your contacts and call history, and lets you save a name for a number.

Information stored on your phone

Contacts, call history, messages you type in the app, your account, your membership key, wallpapers, and settings stay on your phone. CallerLens does not sell this information.

Information shared when you name a caller

If you save a name for a number and mark it safe or not safe, CallerLens sends that phone number, the name, and your choice to a shared directory so another CallerLens account can see the same name. Do not save a name if you do not want that number shared.

Permissions

CallerLens asks to be your Phone app so it can place and answer calls, read your contacts, and read your call history. It uses notifications and the full screen for incoming calls. It does not ask to read your text messages.

Children

CallerLens is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Use the developer email shown on the CallerLens listing in Google Play.
