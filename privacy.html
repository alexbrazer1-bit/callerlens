CallerLens privacy policy

Last updated 7 November 2026.

CallerLens is a phone and messages app. It shows a name for a number, keeps your calls and messages, and lets you mark a number safe or not safe.

Information stored on your phone

Your contacts, call history, text messages, wallpapers, and settings stay on your phone. CallerLens does not sell this information. Your messages are not uploaded to the CallerLens account service.

Premium

CallerLens Premium is a yearly plan. Google Play takes the payment and shows the price in the currency set for that country. Premium stays on the signed-in account until the year ends or you cancel it in Google Play subscriptions. CallerLens does not store your card number.

Your account

If you create an account, CallerLens stores your name, email address, password, and phone number with Supabase so you can sign in on another phone. If you subscribe, the yearly plan is recorded on that account. Supabase is the account service. CallerLens does not sell accounts.

Names you share

If you save a name for a number and mark it safe or not safe, CallerLens sends that phone number, the name, and your choice to the shared CallerLens list. Every signed-in CallerLens account can see that name. Do not save a name if you do not want that number shared. Your email address is not shown with the name.

Permissions

CallerLens asks to be your Phone app so it can place and answer calls, read your contacts, and read your call history. It asks to be your Messages app so it can receive, read, and send text messages, and so a new message can open in CallerLens. It uses notifications and the full screen for incoming calls and new messages. It can see how many SIM cards are in the phone so you can choose which one to use.

What CallerLens does not do

CallerLens does not sell your contacts, calls, or messages. It does not show ads. It does not read your messages in order to build an advertising profile.

Children

CallerLens is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer email shown on the CallerLens listing in Google Play.
